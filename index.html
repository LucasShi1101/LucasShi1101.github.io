Researching transparent and reliable AI

The work

Opening the black box of AI.

I am a researcher at the University of Sydney, advised by Prof. Zhiyong Wang. At the NExT++ Center, I work with Prof. Tat-Seng Chua and Research Fellow Fei Shen.

My work asks how large foundation models compose knowledge, represent concepts at the neuron level, and transform information across their layers.

Research focus

Three questions guide my work.

01

Knowledge composition

How are internal knowledge structures constructed and organized within neural networks?

02

Neuron-level representations

What semantic roles do the “atomic” units of model behavior play?

03

Information transformation

How do models encode, organize, and transform complex information?

Why it matters

Interpretability can make AI more predictable, controllable, and trustworthy.

By tracing the structure of knowledge inside foundation models, I hope to help turn opaque systems into tools that people can understand and guide. That understanding can support safer systems and more reliable creative and reasoning capabilities.
